About Liftlyf

Built for people who want to train consistently.

Keep your workouts organized and build a clear record of your training over time.

Liftlyf gives you a simple place to record workouts and keep your training history in one place.

Log exercises, sets, weights and repetitions, then look back at your previous training sessions whenever you want.

No complicated spreadsheets. No forgotten workouts. Just a simple way to keep track of your training.

Engineering

Built as a real-world engineering project.

Liftlyf combines Android development, backend engineering, APIs, authentication, databases and cloud infrastructure.

Android Application

Built using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with modern Android development tools and local data storage.

Backend Infrastructure

Backend services are built using Spring Boot and deployed using Docker and cloud infrastructure.

Security & APIs

REST APIs, Spring Security and JWT-based authentication support secure communication between systems.

Deployment & Automation

Containerized services and automated workflows help manage builds and deployments.
