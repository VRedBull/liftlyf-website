Privacy

Privacy Policy

Liftlyf is designed to help users track their workouts and fitness activity. We aim to collect and process only the information necessary to provide and improve the app.

Depending on the features you use, Liftlyf may process workout information such as exercises, sets, repetitions and weight data.

We do not sell personal user data. Any future changes involving accounts, cloud synchronization or additional data processing will be reflected in an updated privacy policy.

If you have questions regarding privacy or data, please contact us using the information in the contact section below.
